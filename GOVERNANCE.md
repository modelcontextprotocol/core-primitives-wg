# Core Primitives Working Group Governance

The Core Primitives Working Group follows the shared MCP governance policies:

- [Working and Interest Group governance](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/community/working-interest-groups)
  — participation, membership, decision making, and escalation.
- [MCP Contributor Ladder](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/community/contributor-ladder)
  — organization-wide roles and advancement.

Working Group membership and MCP organization membership are distinct; see the
linked policies for their respective requirements.

The initial work areas are recorded in our [roadmap](./roadmaps/README.md). A link
to the group's charter will be added here when it is published.

## Contributors

Contributors help improve MCP core primitives by joining discussions, sharing
implementation feedback, or submitting an issue or pull request.

## Members

Members are sustained WG contributors recognized through nomination by an existing
WG Member or Lead and review under the
[WG membership process](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/community/working-interest-groups#participation-levels).
Use the [member nomination template](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/core-primitives-wg/issues/new?template=member-nomination.md)
to nominate a contributor.
